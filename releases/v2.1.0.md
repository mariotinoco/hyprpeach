# every desktop at once, with SUPER + TAB

**New plugin: overview.** Press `SUPER + TAB` and every monitor shows all of its desktops at once, as a grid of live pictures — a video playing on another desktop keeps playing in its cell. Click one, or press its number, and the whole desk turns to it. Esc closes it.

    hyprpeach upgrade
    hyprpeach plugin add overview

It needs desktops, which most people already have.

**Also in this release**

- Monitors side by side at the same height are now ordered left to right, so a laptop docked between two screens gets a stable layout.
- On a laptop off its dock, the overview still shows the ten desktops your number keys reach.
- `hyprpeach plugin add` explains what a plugin needs first, instead of adding something that cannot work.
