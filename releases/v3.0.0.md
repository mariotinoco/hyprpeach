# nine desktops, a 3 × 3, and a station in orbit

**The desk is now a 3 × 3.** Nine desktops, and `SUPER + 0` opens the overview — the whole grid at once — just as `SUPER + TAB` does. Desktop 10 is gone, and the number is fixed rather than a setting, because the overview and the new orbit scene are both built on that grid.

**New plugin: orbit.** Your desk as the windows of a station in low orbit: a real-time planet from NASA imagery behind your desktops, one continuous view across every monitor, and every desktop switch a turn around the station. Row by row the sun moves — day, the terminator, night with its city lights.

    hyprpeach upgrade
    hyprpeach plugin add orbit

orbit builds a small renderer from source, so it needs Rust (`mise use -g rust@latest`) and ImageMagick; the first add takes a few minutes and fetches about 85 MB of imagery.

**Fixed:** with one screen held, moving the mouse between monitors no longer flashes a desktop number.

**Upgrading:** your windows stay where they are — every workspace keeps its number. Anything on desktop 10 is no longer on a desktop; move it off first, or gather it afterwards with `hyprctl eval 'require("hyprpeach").gather_rogue_windows()'`. If your own Hyprland config calls `setup()` with `desktop_count`, remove it: 3.0 refuses it rather than ignoring it.
