# ready for Omarchy 4.5

**Fixed:** on Omarchy 4.5, windows could vanish. Omarchy now gives each monitor its own numbered workspaces and re-sorts them at login, on every reload and when a monitor connects. On a hyprpeach desk that put a panel on a workspace that is not a desktop, and anything opened there disappeared at the next switch. hyprpeach now turns that re-sorting off, and leaves the rest of Omarchy alone.

    hyprpeach upgrade

If a window went missing before you upgraded, bring it back with:

    hyprctl eval 'require("hyprpeach").gather_rogue_windows()'

**Fixed:** a held panel always shows its padlock. A hold could be on with nothing on screen saying so, and the desk then looked like paired switching had stopped working. The next switch now brings the padlock back. `SUPER + Y` with the pointer on a panel holds it or lets it go.

**Fixed:** the animated scene is always drawn in front of the wallpaper. Before, a monitor could show the plain wallpaper if the two started at the same moment.

`SUPER + SHIFT + ALT + arrows` now does nothing. In Omarchy 4.5 it moves one monitor's half of a desktop to the other monitor, which tears the desktop in half.

Adding animated now turns off Omarchy's own wallpaper, which sits unseen under the scene and uses video memory. Removing animated turns it back on. If you already have animated, `hyprpeach plugin add animated` turns it off.
