# a desktop is a set of workspaces, one per monitor

Hyprland cannot show one workspace on two monitors at once, so a **desktop** here is a *set* of workspaces — one pinned to each monitor — and every desktop action dispatches once per monitor. Press a key and the whole desk turns.

One rule for the keymap: `SUPER` moves your view, `SUPER + SHIFT` moves the window. Numbers are absolute, arrows are relative; left and right mean desktops, up and down mean panels.

Pure Lua for Hyprland 0.55 or newer — no `hyprpm` plugin, no compiler, no dependencies. Ships an optional Quickshell bar strip and on-screen desktop indicator for Omarchy.
