# every desktop at once

`SUPER + TAB` shows every desktop at once, on every monitor together, each screen showing its own. The pictures are live: a video playing on a desktop you are not on keeps playing in its cell.

- Click a desktop, or press its number (0 is ten), to turn the whole desk to it.
- Esc, `SUPER + TAB` again, or a click between desktops closes it.
- The current desktop is outlined, and a held screen says so.

Tested on two 7680 × 2160 panels on Hyprland 0.56.2. Not there yet: arrow keys, and dragging a window between desktops.
