# a flung window leaves, and you stay where you are

`SUPER + SHIFT + 3` now sends the window to desktop 3 and leaves you on the desktop you were working on. Until now your view went with it. The same goes for every fling: `SUPER + SHIFT + ←` `→` to the desktop beside this one, and `SUPER + SHIFT + ↑` `↓` to the panel above or below.

    hyprpeach upgrade

To go after a window you just sent, press `SUPER` and the same number. To have your view go with it every time, as before, add one line to the `setup()` call in your Hyprland config:

    focus_follows_fling = true,

**Fixed:** an upgrade could leave two bars side by side, every widget drawn twice. The shell was being restarted twice at the same moment, and each restart started one. It is now restarted once. If you have two bars now, `omarchy restart shell` puts it right.

This release was briefly published as 4.0.0. Nothing in it needs you to change anything, so it is 3.1.3, and 4.0.0 has been withdrawn.
