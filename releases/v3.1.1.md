# upgrading from 3.0 keeps your plugins

Upgrading from 3.0.0 to 3.1.0 left the renamed plugins — ports, which was dev-ports, and animated, which was animated-desktops — under their old names until you logged out and back in: the ports widget and the animated background were gone in the meantime. They are now carried over as part of the upgrade itself.

    hyprpeach upgrade

If you already upgraded to 3.1.0 and they went missing, this brings them back; so does logging out and in.
