# desktop tiles centred on the bar

The desktop strip centred itself on the *gutter* — the bar plus the gap Hyprland leaves beside it — instead of on the bar. Symmetric against the window, and five pixels off from every other icon in the panel.

Measured on a 37px bar: Omarchy's tray, clock and status icons all sit on x=7661.5; the strip sat on 7656.

It also stopped deriving its geometry from `general:gaps_out` entirely. A window gap is a number you can set to anything and a bar is 37 pixels wide: at `0` the tiles filled it corner to corner, at `40` the arithmetic went negative and the current tile collapsed to a single dot. Tile thickness and spacing now come from the shell's own scale, so no gap setting can break them.
