# apps that open off the edge are brought back

With the bar down the left edge, apps that put their window beside their tray icon — JetBrains Toolbox is one — put it to the left of the icon, off the edge of the screen: the app is running and its window is open, but nothing appears. Toolbox now opens just right of the bar, at the bottom beside the tray, and stays there every time it is shown. Any other floating window that opens entirely off every monitor is centred on its screen.
